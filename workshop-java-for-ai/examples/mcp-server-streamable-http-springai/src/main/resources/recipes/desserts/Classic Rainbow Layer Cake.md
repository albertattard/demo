# 🌈 Classic Rainbow Layer Cake (6 Layers)

**Makes one 6–8 inch layered cake (12–16 slices)**
Light vanilla sponge + buttery frosting

---

## ✅ Ingredients

### 🎂 Vanilla Cake Batter

* 2½ cups (315 g) all-purpose flour
* 2½ tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt
* 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, softened
* 1¾ cups (350 g) sugar
* 4 large eggs, room temperature
* 1 tbsp vanilla extract
* 1 cup (240 ml) milk, room temperature
* Gel food coloring: **red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple**

---

### 🧈 Vanilla Buttercream Frosting

* 2 cups (450 g) unsalted butter, softened
* 6 cups (720 g) powdered sugar
* 2–3 tbsp milk or cream
* 1 tbsp vanilla extract
* Pinch of salt

---

## 🔥 Oven Setup

* Preheat to **175°C / 350°F**
* Grease & line **6 round pans (6" or 8")**

  > If you only own 2 pans, bake in batches.

---

## 🥣 Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1️⃣ Make the Cake Batter

1. Whisk together:

    * Flour, baking powder, salt
2. In another bowl, cream:

    * Butter + sugar → beat 3–4 minutes until fluffy
3. Add:

    * Eggs one at a time
    * Vanilla extract
4. Alternate adding:

    * Dry mix → milk → dry mix
      Mix just until smooth (don’t overbeat)

---

### 2️⃣ Color the Batter

* Divide batter evenly into **6 bowls**
* Add gel food coloring:

    * Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple
* Mix gently until vibrant

✅ Use **gel colors only**—liquid colors weaken the cake.

---

### 3️⃣ Bake the Layers

* Pour each color into a pan
* Bake **12–15 minutes**
* Toothpick = clean → done
* Cool 10 minutes → remove → cool fully

✅ Layers should be thin, soft, and flat.

---

## 🎂 Buttercream Frosting

1. Beat butter 3–4 minutes until pale & fluffy
2. Add powdered sugar in 3 parts
3. Add:

    * Vanilla, salt, milk
4. Beat 2 more minutes until smooth

✅ Texture should be creamy, not stiff.

---

## 🏗️ Assemble the Rainbow Cake

1. Place **purple layer** on cake board
2. Spread frosting → thin and even
3. Stack in order:

    * Purple → Blue → Green → Yellow → Orange → Red
4. Crumb coat (thin outer layer)
5. Chill 20 minutes
6. Final smooth frosting coat

---

## 🔪 Cutting Tip (Perfect Slices)

* Use a **hot dry knife**
* Wipe between cuts

---

## 🧠 Pro Chef Tips

* Chill layers 30 minutes before stacking → no sliding
* For extra brightness, add **white food gel** to batter before coloring
* If cake domes while baking: trim flat with serrated knife

---

## 🎉 Fun Variations

* **Chocolate Rainbow Cake:** Replace ¼ cup flour with cocoa
* **Rainbow Inside, White Outside:** Classic birthday-style finish
* **Rainbow Cupcakes:** Same batter → bake 16–18 min
