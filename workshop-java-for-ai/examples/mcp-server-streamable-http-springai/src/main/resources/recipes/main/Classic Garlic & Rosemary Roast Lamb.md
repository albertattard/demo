# 🥩 Classic Garlic & Rosemary Roast Lamb

**Best cuts:**

* Leg of lamb (most popular)
* Shoulder (more tender, slightly fattier)

This recipe works for both.

---

## ✅ Ingredients (Serves 4–6)

* 1.8–2.5 kg (4–5.5 lb) **leg or shoulder of lamb**
* 5–6 garlic cloves, sliced
* 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped (or 2 tsp dried)
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1½ tsp salt
* 1 tsp black pepper
* 1 tsp paprika (optional, for color)
* 1 onion, quartered
* 2 carrots, chopped
* 2 celery stalks, chopped
* 1 cup (240 ml) chicken or beef stock
* Optional: 1 lemon (zest + halves)

---

## 🔥 Oven Setup

* Preheat to **220°C / 425°F** (for the first blast)
* After 20 minutes, reduce to **180°C / 350°F**

---

## 🔪 Meat Prep (This Is the Flavor Step)

1. Pat the lamb **dry** with paper towels
2. Make small slits all over the lamb with a knife
3. Push **sliced garlic** into the slits
4. Rub all over with:

    * Olive oil
    * Salt, pepper, paprika
    * Rosemary
    * Lemon zest (if using)

---

## 🥘 Pan Setup

* Place onion, carrot, and celery in the roasting pan
* Pour in **1 cup stock**
* Place lamb on top (fat side up)

✅ This keeps the lamb juicy and makes amazing pan juices for gravy.

---

## ⏱️ Roasting Times (Very Important)

| Doneness | Internal Temp       | Time Per 500 g / 1 lb |
| -------- | ------------------- | --------------------- |
| Rare     | 50–52°C / 122–125°F | 15 min                |
| Medium   | 60–63°C / 140–145°F | 20 min                |
| Well     | 70°C+ / 160°F+      | 25 min                |

✅ For a 2 kg (4.4 lb) lamb at **medium** → about **1 hr 20 min**

---

## 🧈 Roasting Process

1. Roast at **220°C (425°F)** for **20 minutes** → browns the outside
2. Lower to **180°C (350°F)** and continue roasting
3. Baste with pan juices every 25–30 minutes
4. When target temperature is reached:

    * Remove lamb from oven
    * Rest **20 minutes, loosely covered with foil**

⚠️ Do NOT skip resting—this keeps the meat juicy.

---

## 🥄 Quick Pan Gravy (Optional but Recommended)

1. Place roasting pan on stove
2. Remove excess fat
3. Add:

    * 1 cup stock
    * 1 tbsp flour or cornstarch mixed with water
4. Simmer 3–5 minutes → strain → serve

---

## 🍽️ How to Serve

Perfect with:

* Roasted potatoes
* Mint sauce or yogurt sauce
* Green beans or glazed carrots
* Fresh bread

---

## 🧠 Pro Chef Tips

* Shoulder = juicier, more forgiving than leg
* Use a **meat thermometer** for perfect doneness
* If lamb browns too fast, tent loosely with foil
* Slicing across the grain = tender meat
