# 🥥🌶️ Creamy Chickpea & Spinach Coconut Curry (Vegan)

Rich, spiced, protein-packed, and ready in **35 minutes**. Serve with rice or flatbread for a complete meal.

---

## ✅ Ingredients (Serves 4)

* 2 tbsp vegetable or coconut oil
* 1 onion, finely chopped
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
* 2 tsp curry powder
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp paprika
* ½ tsp turmeric
* ½ tsp chili flakes (*Optional*)
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* 2 × 400 g (14 oz) **canned chickpeas**, drained & rinsed
* 1 × 400 ml (14 oz) **full-fat coconut milk**
* ½ cup (120 ml) vegetable stock or water
* 3 cups fresh spinach
* 1 tsp salt (to taste)
* ½ tsp black pepper
* Juice of ½ lime or lemon

---

## 🔥 Step-by-Step Cooking

### 1️⃣ Build the Flavor Base

* Heat oil in a large pan
* Sauté onion for **5–6 minutes** until soft
* Add garlic & ginger → cook **30 seconds**

---

### 2️⃣ Toast the Spices

* Add:

    * Curry powder, cumin, paprika, turmeric, chili flakes
* Stir constantly for **20–30 seconds** (this boosts flavor)

---

### 3️⃣ Create the Sauce

* Stir in tomato paste
* Add chickpeas, coconut milk, and stock
* Simmer gently for **15 minutes**, uncovered

---

### 4️⃣ Finish with Greens

* Stir in spinach → it wilts in **1–2 minutes**
* Add salt, pepper, and lime juice
* Taste and adjust seasoning

---

## 🍽️ How to Serve (Main Course Style)

Serve with:

* ✅ Steamed basmati rice
* ✅ Naan, roti, or flatbread
* ✅ Cucumber salad or pickles on the side

Optional toppings:

* Fresh coriander (cilantro)
* Toasted coconut flakes
* Chili oil

---

## 🧠 Pro Chef Tips

* For **extra richness**, use coconut cream instead of milk
* For **更多 protein**, add cubed fried tofu
* For a **vegetable-loaded version**, add bell peppers or sweet potato

---

## 🔄 Easy Variations

* **Thai-style:** Add lemongrass & a little peanut butter
* **Indian-style:** Add garam masala at the end
* **Low-fat:** Use light coconut milk + more stock
* **Extra spicy:** Add fresh green chilies
