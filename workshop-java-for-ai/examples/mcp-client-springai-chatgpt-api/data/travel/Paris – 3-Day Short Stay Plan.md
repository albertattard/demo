# 🇫🇷 Paris – 3-Day Short Stay Plan

## 🗓️ Day 1 — Historic Core & Eiffel Tower

### Morning

* **Île de la Cité**

    * Notre-Dame (exterior + interior if open)
    * Sainte-Chapelle (stained glass is a must)
* Walk along the **Seine River**

### Lunch

* Café in the **Latin Quarter**

    * Try: Croque monsieur, onion soup, or a light quiche

### Afternoon

* **Louvre Museum**

    * Focus route: Mona Lisa → Venus de Milo → Napoleon Apartments
* Walk through **Jardin des Tuileries**

### Evening

* **Eiffel Tower**

    * Sunset view from:

        * Trocadéro (free) or
        * Summit (ticketed)
* Dinner nearby in **7th Arrondissement**

✅ Key themes: history, landmarks, first-time wow factor

---

## 🗓️ Day 2 — Art, Montmartre & Night Life

### Morning

* **Montmartre**

    * Sacré-Cœur Basilica
    * Place du Tertre (street artists)
* Coffee at a small local café

### Lunch

* Bistro in Montmartre or Pigalle

### Afternoon (Pick one)

* 🎨 **Musée d’Orsay** (Impressionist art)
* 🛍️ **Galeries Lafayette & Opéra Garnier**

### Evening

* Dinner in **Le Marais**
* Optional:

    * Seine River night cruise 🚤
    * Jazz club in Saint-Germain

✅ Key themes: art, romance, nightlife

---

## 🗓️ Day 3 — Local Paris & Relaxed Exploration

### Morning

* **Le Marais**

    * Place des Vosges
    * Boutique shopping
    * Vintage stores & bakeries

### Lunch

* Falafel on **Rue des Rosiers** or casual café

### Afternoon Options (Choose One)

* 🥐 **Pastry + Café Tour**
* 🛍️ **Champs-Élysées shopping**
* 🏰 **Versailles half-day trip** (if you’re ambitious)

### Departure Prep

* Souvenir shopping
* Last café stop

✅ Key themes: local life, food, flexibility

---

# 🏨 Where to Stay (By Vibe)

* **Latin Quarter** – Budget + walkable
* **Le Marais** – Trendy & central
* **Saint-Germain** – Classic Parisian

---

# 🚇 Transportation Tips

* Use a **Navigo Easy** or 10-ticket carnet
* Metro + walking is faster than taxis
* Uber works everywhere

---

# 💶 Daily Budget Estimate (Per Person)

* Food: €35–€70
* Attractions: €20–€40
* Transport: €8–€12
  ➡️ **Total:** ~€65–€120/day
