# 🍅 Classic Tomato Basil Soup (Starter for 4)

Smooth, comforting, and ready in **30 minutes**—a perfect dinner-party starter.

---

## ✅ Ingredients

* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tbsp butter (optional, for richness)
* 1 medium onion, finely chopped
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 kg (2.2 lb) ripe tomatoes, chopped
  *or 2 × 400 g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes*
* 1 tbsp tomato paste
* 1 tsp sugar (balances acidity)
* 2 cups (480 ml) vegetable or chicken stock
* ½ tsp salt (adjust to taste)
* ½ tsp black pepper
* ½ tsp dried oregano or thyme
* ½ cup (120 ml) cream (optional, for creamy version)
* Fresh basil leaves (handful)

---

## 🔥 Cooking Instructions

### 1️⃣ Build the Base

* Heat olive oil + butter in a pot
* Sauté onion on medium heat for **5–6 minutes** until soft
* Add garlic → cook **30 seconds**

---

### 2️⃣ Add Tomatoes

* Stir in:

    * Tomatoes
    * Tomato paste
    * Sugar
    * Salt, pepper, herbs
* Cook uncovered **10–12 minutes**, stirring occasionally

---

### 3️⃣ Add Stock & Simmer

* Pour in stock
* Simmer gently **10 minutes**

---

### 4️⃣ Blend & Finish

* Blend until smooth (stick blender or regular blender)
* Stir in cream (*Optional*)
* Add chopped fresh basil
* Taste and adjust seasoning

---

## 🍽️ How to Serve (Starter Style)

* Serve in **small bowls or cups**
* Garnish with:

    * Basil leaf
    * Drizzle of cream
    * Croutons or garlic bread on the side

---

## 🧠 Pro Chef Tips

* If tomatoes taste too sharp → add **½ tsp honey**
* For ultra-smooth soup → strain after blending
* For deeper flavor → roast tomatoes first (20 min at 200°C / 400°F)

---

## 🔄 Quick Variations

* **Spicy:** Add chili flakes
* **Vegan:** Skip butter & cream
* **Cheesy:** Add grated parmesan at the end
* **Protein boost:** Add blended white beans
