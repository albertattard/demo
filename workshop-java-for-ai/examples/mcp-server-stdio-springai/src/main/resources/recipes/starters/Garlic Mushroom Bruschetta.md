# 🍄 Garlic Mushroom Bruschetta (Starter for 4–6)

Crispy bread, juicy garlic mushrooms, and a hint of lemon—ready in **20 minutes**.

---

## ✅ Ingredients

* 1 baguette or crusty loaf, sliced
* 3 tbsp olive oil
* 250 g (9 oz) mushrooms, finely sliced
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper
* ½ tsp dried thyme or Italian herbs
* 1 tsp lemon juice
* Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
* Optional topping: grated parmesan or crumbled feta

---

## 🔥 Prep & Cook

### 1️⃣ Toast the Bread

* Brush bread with a little olive oil
* Toast in oven at **200°C / 400°F for 6–8 minutes** or pan-toast until golden
* Set aside

---

### 2️⃣ Cook the Mushrooms

1. Heat olive oil in a hot pan
2. Add mushrooms (don’t overcrowd)
3. Cook until golden and moisture evaporates (5–7 min)
4. Add:

    * Garlic
    * Salt, pepper, thyme
5. Cook 30–40 seconds
6. Finish with **lemon juice**

---

### 3️⃣ Assemble

* Spoon hot mushroom mixture onto toasted bread
* Add cheese (*Optional*)
* Garnish with parsley

---

## 🍽️ How to Serve

* Serve **warm**
* Great with:

    * Soup
    * Salads
    * Roast dinners
    * Wine or mocktails

---

## 🧠 Pro Chef Tips

* Don’t salt mushrooms too early—they’ll release too much water
* Hot pan = caramelized flavor
* For luxury version: add **1 tsp butter at the end**

---

## 🔄 Easy Variations

* **Creamy version:** Add 2 tbsp cream at the end
* **Spicy:** Add chili flakes
* **Protein boost:** Add chopped crispy bacon or chicken
