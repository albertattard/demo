## 🍫 Chocolate Chip Muffins (Makes 12)

### ✅ Ingredients

**Dry:**

* 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
* ¾ cup (150 g) sugar
* 2 tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp salt

**Wet:**

* 2 large eggs (room temperature)
* ¾ cup (180 ml) milk
* ½ cup (120 ml) vegetable oil or melted butter
* 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Add-ins:**

* 1 to 1½ cups (170–255 g) chocolate chips (semi-sweet or dark)

---

## 🔥 Oven & Prep

1. Preheat oven to **190°C / 375°F**
2. Line a muffin tin with paper liners (or grease well)

---

## 🥣 Instructions

1. **Mix Dry Ingredients**
   In a large bowl, whisk together:

    * Flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt

2. **Mix Wet Ingredients**
   In another bowl, whisk:

    * Eggs, milk, oil (or butter), and vanilla

3. **Combine (Don’t Overmix!)**

    * Pour wet ingredients into the dry
    * Stir gently until just combined
    * Batter should be slightly lumpy (this keeps muffins soft)

4. **Fold in Chocolate Chips**

    * Save a small handful to sprinkle on top

5. **Fill the Tin**

    * Fill each cup about **¾ full**
    * Sprinkle extra chips on top

6. **Bake**

    * Bake for **18–22 minutes**
    * Toothpick should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs

7. **Cool**

    * Let rest 5 minutes in the pan
    * Transfer to a rack to finish cooling

---

## 🧠 Pro Chef Tips

* **For extra tall muffin tops:** Let the batter rest 10 minutes before baking.
* **For bakery-style flavor:** Use half butter + half oil.
* **Prevent sinking chips:** Toss chocolate chips in 1 tsp flour before adding.

---

## 🧁 Variations (*Optional*)

* **Double chocolate:** Replace ¼ cup flour with cocoa powder
* **Banana chocolate chip:** Add 1 ripe mashed banana
* **Nutty:** Add ½ cup chopped walnuts or pecans
